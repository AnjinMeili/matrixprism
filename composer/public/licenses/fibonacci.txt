Native adaptations use generated golden-angle phyllotaxis coordinates rather
than the board's 128 physical LED map. Point count increases with panel area.
Bloom/chase retain the source's pulse, arm-change, fade and hue structure.
Electric Mandala uses the existing FastLED-compatible inoise8 helper instead
of inoise16, and continuous native coordinates rather than byte-grid folds.
Radial Fire retains 32 heat bands, cooling/spark/transport rules and angular
noise, interpolating bands across native panel pixels. Heat colours use a
continuous black/red/yellow/white gradient. These are adaptations, not
byte-identical firmware ports. FastLED palette stops are interpolated directly
instead of quantized into a 16-entry gradient palette.
